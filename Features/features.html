Create appointments

Quickly and easily create new appointments for your patients, including specifying the date, time, duration, and other details to manage your schedule effectively.

Track Appointments

Keep track of your current, upcoming, and missed appointments to ensure that you're always on top of your schedule and can manage your time efficiently.
